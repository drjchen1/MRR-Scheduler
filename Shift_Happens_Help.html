Shift Happens Handbook

Complete user guide for dynamic scheduling, course coverage enforcement, live roster editing, and letter-sized printing.

1. Start a Session

The start screen focuses only on choosing a source. Operating hours, priorities, and required-course choices come after the roster is loaded.

Use Three Source Files

Standard departmental ingestion using people.csv (unavailability & preferences), teaching.csv (course sections), and mrr.csv (MRR staff).

Upload a Roster

Upload a consolidated mrr_scheduler_input.csv containing all instructors, hours, preferences, and unavailability. Select Try sample schedule for a small, math-themed practice roster.

Build Manually

Start with a clean slate and build your roster, courses, and time constraints manually in the interactive web interface.

Load a Saved Session

Instantly restore a previously saved .json session file to pick up right where you left off with all assignments and configurations preserved.

2. Time Slot & Schedule Customization

Step 3 follows roster review. Set operating hours and scheduling priorities before generating the schedule:

Day & Slot Toggles

Enable or disable specific days (Mon through Sun) and toggle individual 1-hour time slots per day.

Editable Time Chips & Custom Times

Click any existing time chip (e.g. 09:30) to type a new custom time, or click + Add to insert extra time slots.

"Copy to All" Replicator

Quickly replicate one day's customized time slot pattern to all other active days with a single click.

Default Reset

Easily revert to standard departmental weekday hours (Mon–Thu 09:30–16:30, Fri 09:30–12:30) anytime.

Scheduling Priorities

Choose a strategy such as Balanced, Staff Preference Focus, Even Room Load, or Subject Coverage Focus. You can also fine-tune back-to-back preferences, room balance, course variety, and core coverage independently.

3. Data Preview & In-Browser Editor

Step 2 is a roster review. Step 3 combines operating hours, scheduling priorities, and required course coverage before you generate the schedule:

A

Inline Field Editing

Click directly on instructor names, course badges, or required hours to correct typos or modify required hours on the fly.

B

Back-to-Back (B2B) Preference Setting

Choose Yes (prefer consecutive hours), No (spread hours out), or No preference for each staff member.

C

Constraint Management

Add specific unavailable slots (e.g. Tue 10:30) using the dropdown pickers, or click the ✕ on any constraint chip to remove it.

D

Add & Delete Staff

Click Add Staff to insert new instructors or delete existing ones directly from the preview list.

4. Course Coverage & MRR Engine

Choose which courses should be covered in every active time slot. The scheduler uses primary instructors and MRR staff where capacity permits, then reports any remaining gaps:

Core Course Detection

The scheduler automatically detects core courses (e.g., MA 15300, MA 16100, MA 16200, MA 26100) from your dataset.

Custom Required Courses

Type any custom course code (e.g., STAT 30100, MA 16020) to require it across all slots even if no primary instructor teaches it.

Dynamic MRR Claims

If a slot lacks a primary instructor for a required course, MRR staff are automatically assigned and labeled with the missing courses (e.g., MRR / MA 15800, MA 16010).

Coverage Gap Alerts

If a slot has uncovered courses or insufficient MRR capacity, a prominent warning details exactly which slot and courses are missing.

5. Interactive Schedule Grid & Controls

Live Drag & Drop

Drag any staff card into a different slot. Schedule scores, hours, and gap alerts update instantly.

Instant & Modal Swapping

Drag one card on top of another to swap, or open the Swap Staff modal to trade assignments across distant slots. If a negotiated exception is needed, confirm the availability warning to make a manual override.

Constraint Explanations

Before scheduling, Shift Happens identifies impossible hour requests and required courses with no eligible primary or MRR coverage. These are shown as clear schedule alerts rather than hidden failures.

Availability Heatmap

Toggle the heatmap to color-code slots from red (high conflict/low staff depth) to green (abundant staffing).

Full View Mode

Fit the entire weekly schedule onto a single screen without scrolling for clean projector or presentation viewing.

Infinite Undo

Safely revert any move, swap, or manual edit using the Undo button.

Interactive Sidebar Roster

Hover over any staff to see scheduled slot tooltips, conflict tooltips, and cross-highlighting on the grid. Click a grid card to scroll to that staff member in the roster.

6. Schedule Score & Live Metrics

The scheduler computes an overall quality score out of 100 based on four weighted criteria:

Coverage (40%)

Percentage of active slots with all required core courses covered by primary or MRR staff.

Assignments (30%)

Proportion of instructors who have met 100% of their required teaching/MRR hours.

B2B Satisfaction (20%)

Rate of instructors whose back-to-back preferences (consecutive or spaced) are successfully fulfilled.

Balance (10%)

Even distribution of total staffing load across all slots throughout the week.

7. Printing, Saving & Exporting

Print Schedule (Letter Paper)

Click Print Schedule to produce a dedicated, letter-sized (8.5" × 11" landscape) printout formatted to fit on a single page (or minimal pages) with all instructor names and course badges fully legible without truncation.

Save Session (.json)

Export your full schedule, roster, custom courses, and constraints as a .json file to archive or resume editing later. Instructor IDs are saved so people with the same display name remain distinct.

Export Standalone HTML

Generate a self-contained, interactive HTML file with embedded styling, light/dark themes, and built-in print buttons that can be opened on any device.

Dark & Light Theme

Switch between light and dark themes at any time. Print mode automatically optimizes for crisp black-and-white ink efficiency.
